# Limitations and Claim Boundaries

## Evidence limitations

- HHS emPOWER represents Medicare administrative evidence rather than all
  residents or all electricity-dependent medical needs.
- Counts from 1 through 10 are masked as 11, so small values are bounded rather
  than known exactly.
- FEMA National Risk Index values are modeled broad-planning measures, not
  outage forecasts or substitutes for local hazard assessment.
- HPSA records are administrative designations with repeated component rows,
  not a direct count of independent shortages.
- HRSA health-center data do not cover every healthcare facility and do not
  describe backup generation, fuel, storage, load, or continuity capability.
- Eleven county-equivalents are outside automated comparison because source
  geography cannot be reconciled without unsupported allocation.

## Decision limitations

The project identifies robust candidates for further assessment under declared
public-evidence scenarios. It does not establish that a selected county has a
specific engineering need or that a nonselected county lacks need.

`ADVANCE` means robust selection for first-stage assessment under the 20 primary
configurations. `NOT_ADVANCED` means nonselection under those configurations,
not low need, safety, or ineligibility for other programs. `ABSTAIN` preserves
uncertainty rather than forcing a ranking.

## Prohibited interpretations

Results must not be presented as:

- facility-level resilience certification;
- emergency dispatch or response priority;
- a causal estimate of health effects;
- a forecast of outages, equipment failure, or service disruption;
- an engineering, procurement, or funding recommendation;
- a commercial market-size or investment-return estimate; or
- evidence of FEMA, HHS, HRSA, or Census endorsement.
